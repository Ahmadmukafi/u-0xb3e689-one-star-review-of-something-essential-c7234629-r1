One deadpan one-star review of an everyday essential, filed in earnest against a thing that cannot be fixed.

> Sleep: one star. Eight hours of doing nothing and I still woke up tired. Would not recommend.

Sleep is the essential everyone relies on, and the grievance is real: eight hours invested, still tired. The tone stays flat, and the last three words turn it into a review-site punchline.
